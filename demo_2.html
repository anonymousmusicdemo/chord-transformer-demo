Continuous Label Control

Steering the output with emotional attributes

The full model operates with six continuous labels: Arousal, Valence, Danceability, Energy, Instrumentalness, and Liveness. This demo spotlights three representative dimensions to make the control effect easy to hear.

High vs. low contrast pairs
01

Arousal

Measures excitement level. Higher values feel more energetic and active, while lower values feel calmer and more restrained.

Low to high activation
High Arousal
Energetic

Fast-paced and bright in momentum, with a stronger sense of forward motion.

Low Arousal
Calm

More soothing and relaxed, emphasizing gentler movement and lower intensity.

02

Valence

Represents emotional polarity. Positive values tend toward happy or uplifting gestures, while negative values feel sadder or more introspective.

Negative to positive mood
High Valence
Uplifting

A brighter and more optimistic emotional color with an elevated affective tone.

Low Valence
Melancholic

Leans toward sadness and introspection, with a more subdued expressive character.

03

Danceability

Reflects rhythmic suitability for dancing and captures groove, regularity, and body-oriented pulse.

Loose to groove-heavy
High Danceability
Groove-forward

Stronger pulse and clearer rhythmic drive, with a feel that is easier to move to.

Low Danceability
Freer rhythm

Less regular and less groove-centric, creating a looser and more floating rhythmic profile.
