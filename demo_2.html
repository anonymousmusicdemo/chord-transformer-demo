Abstract

Existing transformer-based symbolic music models still struggle to preserve harmonic coherence, long-range structure, and stable expressive characteristics in long-sequence generation. Chord-Label Transformer uses dual conditioning: compact chord-progressions for structure and continuous label embeddings for label conditioning. A chord-aligned fusion design improves harmonic coordination, while Label-Adaptive Layer Normalization reduces label drift. Experiments improve scale consistency, polyphonic quality, and human preference.
